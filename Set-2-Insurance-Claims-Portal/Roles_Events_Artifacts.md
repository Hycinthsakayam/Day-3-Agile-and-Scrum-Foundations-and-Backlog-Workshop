# Roles, events and artifacts

**Roles, events and artifacts used**

*Roles* – **Product Owner** (Head of Claims Operations) owns the ClaimEasy vision and the ordered backlog and decides
priorities between claim types. **Scrum Master** facilitates the events, protects the team from side requests from
branches and removes impediments such as access to the document-storage service. **Developers** (6: front-end, backend,
QA, UX) estimate, plan and build the increment and own the sprint backlog.

*Events* – **Sprint** of 2 weeks. **Sprint Planning** selects stories (e.g. S1, S2, S5, S8 for sprint 1) and creates the
sprint goal "a motor policy holder can register a claim and upload photos online". **Daily Scrum** (15 min) inspects
progress towards the goal. **Backlog refinement** splits large items (the 40-point "any claim type" story) and writes
acceptance criteria. **Sprint Review** demonstrates the increment to claims officers and a few customers. **Retrospective**
picks one or two improvements for the next sprint.

*Artifacts* – **Product Backlog** (14 stories, 55 points) with its commitment, the **Product Goal**: 60% of claims
online in year one. **Sprint Backlog** – the selected stories plus tasks, with the **Sprint Goal** as commitment.
**Increment** – the working portal that meets the **Definition of Done** (attached to the board).
